MLTE is a framework and accompanying technical infrastructure for evaluating machine learning models. At its core is an evaluation-focused domain-specific language (DSL) embedded in Python, used to rigorously document evaluation procedures for ML models with respect to a specific target system.

The associated paper, [*MLTEing Models*](/research/mlteing-models/), was accepted to the New Ideas and Emerging Results (NIER) track at the International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE) 2023.
