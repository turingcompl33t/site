I designed and implemented a software platform for running robust evaluations of agentic cyber capability and propensity under realistic conditions.

On the capability side, I introduced a novel analytic-based scoring mechanism that supports operationally relevant capability evaluations for offensive cyber agents, and designed and implemented complex, dynamic cyber ranges for the agents to operate in.

The evaluations go beyond capability to measure *propensity* under realistic conditions: what an agent is inclined to do, not just what it can do. This is a novel agent property, and one that is critical to operational readiness in military contexts.

Along the way, I established relationships with organizations across the DoW and USG to collaborate on AI evaluation efforts.
