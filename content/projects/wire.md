I serve as the system administrator for WIRE, a multi-tenant compute environment used by research scientists at the Army Cyber Institute and by cadets and faculty at the United States Military Academy.

The role spans infrastructure (servers, storage arrays, and GPUs) and platform services (LLM APIs and computational notebooks), along with authentication, authorization, and observability integration.

I re-architected and re-implemented the system to use Kubernetes as its underlying platform substrate.
